About Demo Hotel

Demo Hotel is a 20-room beachfront property spread across 2 floors in Goa, India. We opened in 2018 and have hosted guests from over 40 countries. Our property blends a relaxed beach-town feel with attentive, personal service.

Facilities

Guests have access to an in-house restaurant serving local and continental cuisine, a conference room suitable for small business meetings and events, free WiFi throughout the property, and complimentary parking. The hotel has 20 rooms across 2 floors.

Rooms and Rates

We offer Standard Rooms with city views starting at ₹4,500 per night, and Deluxe Rooms with sea-facing balconies starting at ₹7,000 per night. All rooms include free WiFi and breakfast.

Policies

Check-in time is 12 PM and check-out time is 11 AM. Free cancellation is available up to 48 hours before check-in; cancellations within 48 hours are charged for one night's stay. Pets are not permitted on the property.
