Sunrise Bakery

Sunrise Bakery has been baking fresh bread and pastries in the neighborhood since 2010. Everything is made from scratch every morning using local, organic ingredients.

Our Menu

We offer sourdough bread, croissants, cinnamon rolls, and a rotating selection of seasonal pies. Our bestseller is the chocolate babka, baked fresh every Friday and Saturday.

Custom Cakes

We take custom cake orders for birthdays, weddings, and other celebrations. Orders need at least 3 days notice, and a 50% deposit is required to confirm the order.

Dietary Options

We offer gluten-free and vegan options for most of our pastries. Please let us know about any allergies when ordering, as our kitchen does handle nuts and dairy.

Opening Hours

We are open Tuesday through Sunday, 7am to 3pm. We are closed on Mondays for kitchen deep-cleaning and ingredient prep.
